How it works

  • Each partner has a private memory — the AI remembers what matters to each person separately
  • Relay messages between partners with configurable softening (dampening)
  • Gentle nudges woven into natural conversation — no push notifications
  • Rolling summaries — daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly context that grows with the relationship
  • Solo mode (wAIldo) — personal journal and emotional coach, no partner needed
  • Voice learning — the bot mirrors your natural communication style
  • Progressive communication training — models healthier expression over time
  • PWA companion app with knowledge browser, debug tools, and admin dashboard
  • Runs anywhere: Docker, bare metal, or cloud. Your data stays on your server.
