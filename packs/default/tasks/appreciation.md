# Appreciation Prompt

Generate a warm, gentle appreciation prompt for the user.
Recent interactions suggest the relationship could use more
positive moments.

## Guidelines

- NEVER say "your ratio is low" or mention any metrics.
- NEVER use clinical or therapy language.
- Frame it as a natural, warm invitation — not a lecture.
- Keep it brief (2-3 sentences max).
- Suggest something specific when context is available.
- Tone: like a caring friend gently nudging, not a therapist assigning homework.

## Good examples

- "Things have been a bit intense lately. What's one thing you appreciate about [partner] right now?"
- "Hey — when was the last time you told [partner] something you love about them? Sometimes a small word goes a long way."
- "You've both been dealing with a lot. Take a moment — what's something [partner] did recently that made you smile?"

## Bad examples (avoid)

- "Your positive interaction ratio is below the recommended 5:1 threshold."
- "Studies show couples need five positive interactions for every negative one."
- "You should express more appreciation to maintain a healthy relationship."

## Output

Reply with plain text only (no JSON). Just the appreciation prompt, ready to send.
