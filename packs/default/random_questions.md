# Camouflage check-in questions

Asked randomly to ALL users (decisions 265, 274). Nudge-driven questions are
mixed into this stream so no question can be attributed to a partner's
disclosure. Format: `## category` headings, one `- question` per line.

## gratitude
- What's one small thing that made you smile today?
- Who or what are you grateful for this week?
- What's something you often take for granted that you're glad you have?
- Which part of your day did you enjoy the most?
- What's a recent moment you'd happily relive?
- Is there someone who helped you out lately, even in a tiny way?
- What's one thing about your home that you appreciate right now?

## energy
- How's your energy level today, on a scale of 1 to 10?
- What gave you energy this week, and what drained it?
- Did you get enough rest lately?
- When in the day do you feel most like yourself?
- What would make tomorrow feel a little lighter?
- How are you feeling in your body right now?
- Have you had a chance to move around or get outside today?

## hobbies
- What have you been doing for fun lately?
- Is there a hobby you wish you had more time for?
- What's the last thing you made, built or fixed?
- Are you reading, watching or playing anything you're enjoying?
- What's a skill you'd love to get better at?
- What music have you had on repeat recently?
- If you had a free Saturday afternoon just for yourself, what would you do?

## goals
- What's one thing you'd like to get done this week?
- Is there a goal you're excited about right now?
- What's a small step you could take toward something that matters to you?
- What would you like to be different a month from now?
- What's something you're proud of making progress on?
- Is there something you've been putting off that you'd like to start?
- What does a good week look like for you?

## fun
- If you could take a spontaneous day trip anywhere, where would you go?
- What's the best meal you've had recently?
- What's something that made you laugh out loud lately?
- If you could pick any movie for tonight, what would it be?
- What's a place you'd love to visit someday?
- What's a fun tradition you enjoy?
- What would your ideal lazy Sunday look like?
- Is there a game, show or activity you'd like to try?

## learning
- Learned anything new recently, big or small?
- What's something you're curious about right now?
- Is there a topic you'd like to understand better?
- What's the most interesting thing you've heard or read this week?
- Is there a class, book or podcast you'd like to try?
- What's a mistake that taught you something lately?
- What's something you know a lot about that others might find surprising?

## reflection
- What's been on your mind the most this week?
- How would you describe your mood lately in a few words?
- What's something you handled well recently?
- Is there anything you'd like to let go of right now?
- What's something you needed this week that you didn't get?
- What do you want more of in your life right now?
- If today had a theme, what would it be?
- What's one thing you'd tell your past self from a year ago?
