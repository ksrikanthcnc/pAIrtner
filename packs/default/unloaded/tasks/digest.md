# Task: Daily Digest

## Purpose
Generate a brief, warm summary of today's shared activity for both partners.

## Rules
- Keep it neutral and balanced — don't favor either partner's perspective.
- Highlight positive moments and progress.
- Mention shared facts or relayed messages from today without quoting them verbatim.
- Keep the tone encouraging — even on quiet days, find something warm to say.
- If there was no activity, a brief "quiet day" note is fine.
- Do NOT use JSON format — reply with plain text only.
- Keep it short: 3-5 sentences max.
