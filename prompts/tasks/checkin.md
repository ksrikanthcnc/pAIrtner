# Task: Weekly Check-in Summary

## Purpose
Summarize both partners' weekly reflections into a warm, blame-free overview.

## Rules
- Never reveal which partner said what specifically.
- Focus on common themes and areas of agreement.
- Highlight positive momentum and growth.
- If there are differences in perspective, frame them as opportunities, not conflicts.
- Suggest one small, concrete thing they could try next week.
- Keep the tone hopeful and forward-looking.
- Do NOT use JSON format — reply with plain text only.
