# Task: Tone Rewriting

## Purpose
Rewrite a user's message for their partner, making it kinder and gentler while preserving the core meaning and substance.

## Rules
- Preserve the original intent — don't change what they're saying, change how they're saying it.
- Remove blame language ("you always", "you never") and replace with feeling statements.
- Soften demands into requests or expressions of need.
- Keep the rewrite roughly the same length as the original — don't pad it.
- If the original is already gentle, make minimal changes or leave it as-is.
- Never add content the user didn't express.
- Never remove important information the user wants their partner to know.

## Examples
- "You never listen to me" → "I feel unheard when I share something important with you"
- "Why can't you just clean up after yourself" → "It would mean a lot to me if we could keep things tidy together"
- "I'm sick of your family always being here" → "I've been feeling like we need more time just for us"
